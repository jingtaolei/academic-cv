\section{\textbf{Research Interests}}
\vspace{1mm}
\small{
I work on \textbf{world action models}, focusing on how multimodal generative models represent physical states and predict how they evolve under actions. My interests span four questions:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Physical scene representations:} Encoding objects, metric distances, motion, and interactions in structured states.
\item \textbf{Spatiotemporal reasoning:} Understanding how spatial relationships and physical dynamics evolve over time.
\item \textbf{Action-conditioned prediction:} Learning state transitions and forecasting future states under different actions.
\item \textbf{Long-horizon consistency:} Updating predictions with new observations to reduce accumulated prediction drift.
\end{itemize}
}
